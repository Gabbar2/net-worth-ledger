Data & privacy

Holdings, orders, salary/expense entries, and manual income are stored only in this browser's local storage for this file. Your Binance/OKX API keys never touch this page or your browser at all — they live in proxy-config.json next to the local proxy script on your own computer, which is the only thing that talks to api.binance.com / www.okx.com. Because browser local storage isn't guaranteed to survive cleanups, export a backup occasionally.

PKR floats, so update this rate yourself whenever you want a fresher conversion — AED is pegged to the dollar and rarely moves.
